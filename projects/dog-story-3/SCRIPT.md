## Sound

All barks and effects are synthesized (`tools/synth.py`, now with a foley section: thunder, door bang
and slam, bowl clonk, cha-ching, drumroll, marker squeak, page flips, phone notification, brake squeal,
slide whistle, birds, kibble crunch, gulp). Score: store muzak that is muffled behind the door and
opens up while it is open, rain and sad piano, a curious pluck when he finds TJR, sped-up lofi with a
racing clock for the time-lapse, an orchestral hit under the howl, a sappy violin for the begging, a
boom-bap montage that drops out for TJR's line, a trap swagger beat for the drive, a tape stop for
"B-Brodie?!", a ta-da for the check, a bouncy groove cut by a record scratch, and an outro. Music
ducks under every bark and under TJR's voice.

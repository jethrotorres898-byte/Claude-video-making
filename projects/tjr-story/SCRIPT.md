# "The Student & The Teacher": a stickman trading story

Format: vertical 9:16 (1080x1920), 30 fps, about 40 seconds.
Style: same look as the reference clip. Black background, white stickman,
candlestick charts, glowing BUY / SELL buttons, bold white captions with gold
highlight words.

Captions carry the story on screen (no voiceover). Words in **bold** are gold.

| Time | Scene | On-screen caption | What happens |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.0 – 2.4 | The struggle | HE TRADED **EVERY DAY** | Stickman hammers BUY / SELL, chart chops around, balance ticking down from $10,000. |
| 2.4 – 4.6 | | AND **LOST** EVERY DAY | A huge red candle crashes the chart. Screen shakes, his face drops. |
| 4.6 – 7.0 | | ANOTHER **BLOWN** ACCOUNT | Balance hits $2,140 in red. He slumps over in the chair. |
| 7.0 – 9.5 | The break | SO HE TOOK A **BREAK** | The chart fades away. He sighs, leans back and picks up his phone. |
| 9.5 – 12.4 | The discovery | JUST **SCROLLING...** | Bored face. He flicks past "GET RICH IN 1 DAY", "COPY MY TRADES", "100X SIGNALS" posts. |
| 12.4 – 16.0 | | UNTIL HE FOUND **TJR** (sub: a day trader who teaches people how to trade) | Feed stops on TJR's post. His eyes go wide, he hits FOLLOW. |
| 16.0 – 19.6 | The grind | HE STUDIED **EVERY GUIDE** | At his desk watching TJR's videos, taking notes. Checklist pops in: risk management, patience, a real trading plan, no FOMO. |
| 19.6 – 21.8 | | **DAY** AFTER **DAY** | Calendar flips DAY 1 → DAY 30. |
| 21.8 – 23.0 | | **ONE MONTH** LATER... | |
| 23.0 – 26.2 | The win | 100% **DISCIPLINE** | Same chart setup as the opening (callback to "0% DISCIPLINE"). He's calm, waits for the setup, presses BUY once. |
| 26.2 – 30.0 | | HIS FIRST **BIG WIN** | Green candles rip upward, +$4,820. He jumps out of his chair, confetti. |
| 30.0 – 32.6 | The thank you | THEN HE MET THE MAN WHO **TAUGHT HIM** | He walks up to TJR in person. |
| 32.6 – 36.0 | | THE STUDENT MET **THE TEACHER** | Handshake. Speech bubble: "THANK YOU, TJR. YOUR VIDEOS CHANGED EVERYTHING." |
| 36.0 – 38.2 | Outro | EVERY **PRO** WAS ONCE A **BEGINNER** | Stickman stands tall in front of a rising green chart. |
| 38.2 – 40.5 | | STAY DISCIPLINED. **KEEP GOING.** | Fade out. |

## Sound

Original soundtrack generated in code (`music.py`): tense and sparse while he's losing,
quiet during the break, building through the study montage, a riser into the BUY
click, a full drop on the win, and a warm finish for the thank-you. Sound effects:
button clicks, the crash, scroll ticks, a discovery chime, a cash-register hit and
whooshes on scene changes.

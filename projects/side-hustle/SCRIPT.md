# "The Side Hustle": neon-noir trailer

Format: vertical 9:16 (1080x1920), 30 fps, about 52 seconds.

## The style ("Neon Noir", `lib/noir.js`)

- Characters are solid shadow silhouettes with a glowing neon rim (cyan at the job,
  gold once he finds TJR, magenta in the city). Faces only appear when a light hits them:
  the fluorescent tube, the laptop, a neon sign.
- Cinematic finish on every frame: letterbox bars, vignette, film grain, light rays,
  bokeh, rain, parallax skylines, a gold light sweep on the title card.
- Holographic UI for the search bar, results, TJR's live trading and his channel.
- Subtitles instead of pop captions: phrase by phrase, words brighten as they're spoken,
  key words in gold, the hero's own lines in cyan quotes.
- TJR is a gold-rimmed silhouette with his swept-back hair, goatee and chain.
- Luxury items are kept generic (no real brand logos).

## Voices

Kokoro-82M: narrator `am_onyx` (the deepest voice, ~86 Hz) at 0.95 speed, hero `am_puck`.
Every line is checked with Whisper, on its own and over the final mix.

## Score

Drones, a ticking clock and fluorescent hum at the job; a pulsing synth for the search;
taiko + string ostinato when he finds TJR; hits on each word of the grind; a braam for
"One year later"; then the epic drop (taiko, kick, choir, 808) for the payoff, with a braam
on "a month". Ducked under the voices, with extra dips where a line sits on a big hit.

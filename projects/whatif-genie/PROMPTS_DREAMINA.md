# Short prompts for Dreamina

Dreamina is an image generator, not a chat: keep prompts short, upload the reference with the Reference button,
and set the aspect ratio to 16:9 in the settings (not in the prompt).

References: `refs/clyde_ref.png` (Clyde full body), `refs/clyde_mogger_ref.png` (Mogger face), and the genie sheet you make with prompt 0.

## 0. Genie character sheet (make first)

Reference: refs/clyde_ref.png (for the style)

```
Hand-drawn ink cartoon, thick rough black outlines, flat muted colours, character sheet on cream paper. A funny, proud turquoise genie with a red turban with a gold jewel, big curly black moustache, small pointed beard, purple vest, gold bracelets, smoky tail coming out of a golden lamp. One full-body pose plus face expressions: proud, shocked, angry, stern, happy, scared.
```

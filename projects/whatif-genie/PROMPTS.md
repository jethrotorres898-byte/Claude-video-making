# Image prompts: "What If... a genie gave you only ONE wish?"

Use with ChatGPT image generation, Gemini, or Midjourney. Upload Clyde's character sheet (and the genie
sheet once you have it) with every prompt. Each prompt repeats the style line so it works on its own.

**Style line (already included below):** Hand-drawn digital ink illustration in the exact same style as the
attached Clyde character sheet: thick, slightly rough black outlines, flat muted colours with light texture,
full detailed background, 16:9 landscape, no text, no speech bubbles.

**Setting:** Clyde's cosy living room: a worn sofa, a window with curtains, a round rug, a small side table.

## 0. Genie character sheet (make this first)
Character sheet in the exact same hand-drawn ink style as the attached Clyde sheet, cream paper background. A funny, pompous genie: tall turquoise skin, red turban with a gold jewel and a white feather, huge curly black moustache, small pointed beard, gold hoop earrings, purple open vest, gold bracelets, a smoky turquoise tail rising out of a golden oil lamp instead of legs. Show a full-body pose and expression heads: proud/grand, lecturing with one finger up, shocked, furious (red face, steam from ears), stern with arms crossed, blissful eyes-closed smile, horrified with hands on cheeks.

## 1. The hook
Hand-drawn digital ink illustration in the exact same style as the attached Clyde character sheet: thick, slightly rough black outlines, flat muted colours with light texture, full detailed background, 16:9 landscape, no text, no speech bubbles. Clyde sits on the sofa in his cosy living room, curiously rubbing a dusty old golden oil lamp, one eyebrow raised.

## 2. POP
[style line]. The genie bursts out of the golden lamp in a huge puff of white smoke, arms spread wide, grinning proudly. Clyde falls backwards off the sofa in shock, eyes popping, hair standing on end.

## 3. "You get ONE wish!"
[style line]. The genie floats proudly above his lamp holding up one finger, little sparkles around his hand. Clyde stands in front of him, looking up with an excited open-mouthed smile.

## 4. Clyde turns to the camera
[style line]. Close-up of Clyde turning to look straight at the camera with a sly scheming face, half-closed eyes, wide toothy grin, fingers steepled under his chin. The genie is out of focus in the background.

## 5. "The rules are—"
[style line]. The genie lectures with one finger raised, unrolling a very long scroll of rules that spills onto the floor. Clyde smirks and raises one finger to interrupt him.

## 6. "TIME STOP" (Mogger)

## 7. "WHAT?! I haven't finished the rules yet!"

## 8. "TA-IME... STOP" (Mogger, more dramatic)
[style line]. Extreme close-up of Clyde's Mogger face at a dramatic tilted angle, even more intense and smug, sparkles, the living room blurred behind him.

## 9. "NO!"
[style line]. The genie is furious: bright red face, steam blasting out of his ears, mouth wide open shouting, fists clenched, turban slightly askew.

## 10. "MIND CONTROL"
[style line]. Clyde grins smugly and stretches his hand toward the genie; a swirling purple hypnotic spiral flows out of his palm.

## 11. "I cannot give you that..."
[style line]. The genie floats with his arms firmly crossed, eyes closed, nose in the air, refusing. Clyde gives him a skeptical, unimpressed look.

## 12. "Okay... then I wish for a random ability"

## 13. "Your wish is my..."
[style line]. The genie happily casts magic with his eyes closed and a blissful smile; golden sparkles stream from his hands toward Clyde. Clyde winks at the camera.

## 14. "TIME STOP!" (cutting in)

## 15. "...command."
[style line]. The genie finishes with a grand bow, one hand on his chest, eyes still closed, totally unaware. A glowing golden clock appears above Clyde's head; Clyde looks smug.

## 16. "Wait... NOOO!"
[style line]. The genie realises in horror: hands pressed to his cheeks, eyes huge, mouth stretched long. Clyde grins wickedly inside a glowing clock-shaped aura.

## 17. Time stops
[style line]. The whole living room is frozen and drained to grey: the genie frozen mid-scream, a fly frozen in mid-air, sweat drops hanging still. Only Clyde is in full colour, tiptoeing past with a finger on his lips.

## 18. Evil laugh
